Branch procedure

Snapcheck workflow

Phase One

Supervisor

  • Choose a snapcheck from the assigned list.
  • Select the staff member from the branch directory by email.
  • Set the submission timeline and issue or re-issue the task.
  • Send an email alert with the snapcheck name, guide and deadline.

Phase Two

Snapchecker

  • Open the assigned guide and review the listed checks.
  • Confirm the registers, dual control, key custody and annual swaps.
  • Capture findings and attach support documents if required.
  • Submit the completed report back to the supervisor.

Phase Three

Supervisor review

  • Review the input findings and supporting attachments.
  • Approve, complete or re-issue for correction with comments.
  • Store the final record for future audit and review.

Sample procedure

Strong room keys & combinations control.

SNAP CHECKS NAME

STRONG ROOM KEYS & COMBINATIONS

SNAP CHECKS REF

SNAPCHECK-001

WHAT TO CHECK

  • Check the register to confirm that all handovers and changes have been documented as required.
  • Confirm dual control through a physical check and compare with the keys register.
  • Confirm there is no compromise: the person holding the key must not hold the combination, and vice versa.
  • Confirm the authorized person keeps the key register under lock and key.
  • Confirm annual swapping of duplicate keys.

GUIDANCE

Below the guidance, the snapchecker must record findings and attach any supporting documents before submitting to the supervisor.
